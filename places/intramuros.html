Intramuros

Intramuros is a historic walled area within the city of Manila, Philippines. It is known for its Spanish colonial architecture, old stone walls, and rich history that reflects the country’s past. Visitors can explore various landmarks, museums, churches, plazas, and historical sites within the walls of Intramuros. The area is also a popular destination for tourists who want to experience the culture, heritage, and unique atmosphere of old Manila. Walking around its narrow streets allows visitors to appreciate the preserved buildings and learn more about the Philippines during the Spanish colonial period.
